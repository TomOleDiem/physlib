# Goal: infinite-volume dynamics of the Bose-Hubbard model with pairing

Status file for the thermodynamic-limit construction. Code lives under
`PhyslibAlpha/QuantumMechanics/InfiniteBosons/`. Nothing here is done until it builds without
`sorry` and passes the linters.

## Target theorem

Setting.

- `L` a lattice with a `SimpleGraph` of degree at most `d`. Bonds are the edges.
- Hamiltonian `H = D(N) + B`.
  - `D = Σ_x D_x(N_x)` with arbitrary real functions `D_x : ℕ → ℝ`. This covers
    `U_x N_x(N_x-1) - μ_x N_x` with no sign condition on `U`.
  - `B = Σ_{x~y} (h_xy a_x† a_y + Δ_xy a_x† a_y† + h.c.)` with `|h_xy|, |Δ_xy| ≤ K`.
    Translation-invariant pairing is allowed, so particle number is not conserved.
- Local observables: the net `tensorNet (fun _ => ℕ)`, `A(X) = B(ℓ²(ℕ^X))`. The theorem is stated
  first for the local algebra `A_fin(X)` generated by operators of finite occupation transfer
  (finite linear combinations of matrix units `|m⟩⟨n|` in the occupation basis), and then for its
  norm closure.
- Regular states: locally normal states `ω` of the net with uniform local moments
  `sup_x ω((N_x+1)^p) ≤ M` for some `p > p₀(d)`.

Statement.

1. Existence. For every regular `ω`, finite `X`, `A ∈ A(X)` and `t`, the finite-volume values
   `ω(α_t^{Λ}(A))` converge as `Λ ↑ L` (with the occupation truncation removed). The limit
   `ω_t(A)` is uniform for `t` in compact sets.
2. Independence. The limit does not depend on the exhaustion.
3. Regularity is preserved. `ω_t` is a regular state with
   `sup_x ω_t((N_x+1)^p) ≤ e^{c_p|t|} M`, where `c_p` depends only on `p`, `d`, `K`.
4. Group property. `(ω_t)_s = ω_{t+s}` and `ω_0 = ω`.
5. Continuity. `t ↦ ω_t(A)` is continuous.
6. Consistency. For `Δ = 0` the dynamics agrees with the number-conserving construction
   (Deuchert-Lampart-Lemm, Buchholz algebra branch `claude/buchholz-import`).

Representation level, after 1 to 5. For an invariant regular state, the dynamics is implemented in
the GNS representation by a strongly continuous unitary group. Stone's theorem then gives the
generator.

## Known risk

Milestones 4 to 7 with pairing are not in the literature. The published construction uses
particle-number conservation for the cutoff-locality estimate. The moment bound of milestone 3 is
the reason to expect the extension to work. If an estimate fails, it is recorded here with the
failing step, not replaced by a hypothesis.
